---

## 💡 What is RecipeBook?

RecipeBook helps users turn available ingredients into **quick, practical recipes** instead of wondering *“What can I cook with this?”*

Simply enter your ingredients and discover suitable recipes through a clean, easy-to-use interface.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 🥕 **Ingredient-based recipe discovery**
- 🔎 **Smart recipe search**
- ❤️ **Save your favourite recipes**
- 📱 **Responsive & intuitive UI**
- 📖 **Detailed recipe instructions**
- ⚡ **Fast, lightweight experience**
- 🔄 **Single deployed application** — React frontend + FastAPI backend

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

**Frontend:** React.js · Vite · CSS  
**Backend:** FastAPI · Python · Uvicorn  
**Deployment:** Render

---

## 🔄 How It Works

**Enter Ingredients → Find Matching Recipes → Explore → Cook 🍳**

---

## 🎯 Problem We Solve

People often have ingredients at home but struggle to decide what to make with them. RecipeBook reduces this friction by helping users quickly discover recipes based on what they already have.
